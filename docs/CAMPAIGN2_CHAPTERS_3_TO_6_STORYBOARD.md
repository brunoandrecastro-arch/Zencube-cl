# ZenCube 3D · Campaign 2.0 – Chapters III–VI production storyboards

> **STORYBOARD ONLY · NOT A FINISHED GAME BUILD.** These are authored narrative beats and suggested English speech bubbles; the actual 120 illustrated pages, newly required audio and new HD arena/skin assets are not yet made.

Established chapters remain **I Shadow Realm** (30 pages) and **II Abyss of Memories** (30 pages). Their saves and gates must stay unchanged. The continuing chapter order is III Ember Temple → IV Celestial Realm → V Shattered Moon Realm → VI Satori Realm.

### Gameplay invariants
- Each chapter has 30 ordered Manhwa pages, 3 genuine native 4×4 boss fights, 96 legal cells / 204 win lines, and boss-specific portraits, arenas, music and 3D cube skin.
- Reuse existing 3+3 Realm Chronicles boss ids and original OGG for Ember, Celestial and Satori where available; add three new bosses and four original tracks for Shattered Moon.
- Only a real boss victory unlocks subsequent story pages; loss or draw allows retries. Save each chapter independently, including partial match.
- To unlock the next chapter require all prior 30 pages, 3/3 defeated bosses and 3/3 normal 4×4 Realm achievements, with one-time glass-shatter only at actual Realm transfer.
- Render the cube as a central narrative symbol without ever changing actual 4-in-a-row game rules. No silently substituted pictures or copied songs.
