# Campaign 2.0 final guardian series
First two guardians remain single-win. Final guardian uses first to two decisive wins, draws do not score. At 0:2 or 1:2 the player explicitly retries a new series. The board and the score persist together in the existing version-1 chapter save; legacy already-defeated guardians stay defeated. Music is not restarted between rounds. The shared configuration uses Medium, Hard, Master, Hard, Grandmaster (Shadow, Abyss, Ember, Celestial, Satori). Only Shadow and Abyss have native 30-page Campaign 2.0 controllers on this branch; future chapters must use the same shared rules. Original Realm Chronicles archive remains unchanged.
